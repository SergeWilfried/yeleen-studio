# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Creators, small brands and agencies in Francophone Africa (Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Sénégal first) who make video, images and voice-overs with AI. Most are on a phone, often on mobile data, and pay with Mobile Money (Orange Money, Moov Money, Wave), not a card. The interface is in French. They know what they want to make and judge the tool by how fast a first result appears.

## Product Purpose
Studio Yeelen is an AI creative studio. Cinema, the first tool, turns an idea or a few photos into short videos, either step by step (image, motion, montage) or through ready-made templates (Modèles). Image, Audio, the Bibliothèque and the Spot pub screen follow. Credits are called cauris and are topped up with Mobile Money.
Success: a creator signs in, tops up a small amount, and gets a result they can post the same day, without needing to understand models or settings.

## Brand Personality
Cinematic, warm, direct. The name is Bambara for "light" and the mark is a camera iris with a lime opening. Confident without hype: plain French, concrete promises, prices always shown before a run.

## Anti-references
Not a generic SaaS template (identical icon cards, gradient text, hero-metric blocks). Not a Western AI-tool clone that assumes a card, a fast connection and English. Not a wall of model names: templates are named for what the creator gets (« Retrouvailles », « Mon logo »), not for the model behind them.

## Design Principles
1. The creator's result comes first: media and previews lead, settings stay out of the way.
2. Say the price before the run, in cauris, and say what happens if it fails (refund).
3. Built for a phone on mobile data: light assets, lazy loading, layouts that work at 360 px.
4. Mobile Money is a first-class flow: clear steps, honest waiting states, recoverable errors.
5. One loud accent (lime) so the primary action is unmistakable; every control has all of its states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, visible keyboard focus, keyboard-operable dialogs, tabs and menus, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured, touch targets at least 44 px. French copy throughout; screen readers must not be flooded by countdowns or progress ticks.
