# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Creators, marketers and brands who want to generate video, images and audio with AI. They arrive from ads or search, skim fast, and decide in seconds whether to sign up. This is a faithful replica of the Higgsfield homepage built as a client/demo mockup.

## Product Purpose
Landing page of an AI creative studio (video, image, audio). Success: the visitor understands the toolset at a glance, is pulled in by motion-forward media, and clicks "sign up / get my discount".

## Brand Personality
Bold, cinematic, fast. Media-first, high contrast, energetic, confident.

## Anti-references
Not a generic SaaS template (identical icon cards, gradient text, hero-metric blocks). Not a clone with visible rough edges: where the Higgsfield original is weak (low contrast, missing focus states, poor mobile behaviour) this version should be better. (Inferred from the interview; not explicitly confirmed.)

## Design Principles
1. Match the reference's structure and feel, but fix its weak spots.
2. Media carries the page: posters first, motion on intent, never heavy by default.
3. One loud accent (lime) used sparingly so the primary action is unmistakable.
4. Fast by construction: lazy mounting, light assets, no layout shift.
5. Every interactive element has all of its states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, visible keyboard focus, keyboard-operable cards and carousels, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured (no autoplay video when set), touch targets at least 44px.
